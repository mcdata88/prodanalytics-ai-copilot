## Deep Dive: Primary Anomaly & Root Cause

- **Affected Cohort:** Log Management, Mid-Market tier. **34 of the 131** accounts provisioned on the product dropped more than 15% WoW. [Q6]

  | Movement | Accounts | Prior → Current GB | Δ | ARR | 90-day renewals | At Risk / Critical |
  | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
  | Paused (<10% of prior) | 11 | 14,215 → 296 | −97.9% | $3.07M | 7 ($2.06M) | 4 / 3 |
  | Declined >15% | 23 | 28,476 → 14,503 | −49.1% | $4.50M | 12 ($2.75M) | 14 / 5 |

  | Grew 5–15% | 6 | 7,416 → 7,919 | +6.8% | $1.44M | 0 | 1 / 1 |

- **Quantitative Signal:** This is a **single-week step change, not a trend**. Mid-Market Log Management ingestion held between 152.2K and 153.9K GB for the five weeks before 2026-11-02, then fell to 126.2K GB. Adoption fell from 88.55% to 80.92%, which is 116 down to 106 adopted accounts out of 131. That matches the 11 paused accounts dropping below the 25 GB adoption threshold. [Q7]
- **Revenue Exposure:** The 34 declining accounts hold **$7.57M ARR**. **19 of them ($4.81M) renew within 90 days.** **26 of the 34** are already flagged At Risk or Critical by CSMs, compared with 13 of the 91 stable accounts. [Q6]
- **Qualitative Evidence (Support Tickets):** In the 14 days before the as-of date there were **25 Log Management tickets: 24 from Mid-Market and 1 from Enterprise.** Of the 34 declining accounts, **22 filed a ticket**, and all 22 mention overage or the log bill. 6 of them use renewal, churn or alternatives language. [Q8] Pain points:
  1. **Cutting or pausing log shipping (12 tickets).** Example, TKT-1059: *"overage bill this month was nearly 3x our committed spend. We have paused log shipping from staging and non-prod until pricing is sorted."* The others are TKT-1060, -1066, -1067, -1070, -1079, -1082, -1087, -1089, -1091, -1093 and -1098.
  2. **Sampling or throttling to stay under quota (4 tickets).** Example, TKT-1063: *"We are sampling logs at 20% now just to stay within the committed tier."* The others are TKT-1068, -1069 and -1086.
  3. **Retention tiering or cheaper cold storage (4 tickets).** Example, TKT-1071: *"7 days hot, 90 days cold - at a lower price point. Right now overages on 30-day retention are forcing us to drop logs entirely."* The others are TKT-1077, -1085 and -1088.
  4. **Surprise bills with no budget caps (2 tickets).** Example, TKT-1080: *"we only found out about the overage when the invoice arrived."* The other is TKT-1100.

  The remaining 2 Mid-Market tickets, about SCIM/SSO (TKT-1081) and cost breakdown (TKT-1084), are not related to the drop. [Q9]
- **Conclusion:** Mid-Market customers are deliberately cutting ingestion in response to Log Management overage pricing. This is not a product defect or a telemetry gap. Enterprise and SMB volume did not move (+0.12% and +0.25%). 12 of the 34 declining accounts have not filed a ticket, so for those accounts the reason is not confirmed.

**Q3: Portfolio scorecard, latest week vs prior week.** 4 rows.
```sql
WITH wk AS (SELECT MAX(week_start) AS cur, MAX(week_start) - INTERVAL 7 DAY AS prv FROM weekly_product_usage),
m AS (
  SELECT u.product_name, u.week_start,
    ROUND(100.0 * COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN u.is_adopted = TRUE THEN u.account_id END) / NULLIF(COUNT(DISTINCT a.account_id), 0), 2) AS adoption,
    ROUND(SUM(u.ingestion_gb), 2) AS gb
  FROM weekly_product_usage u JOIN accounts a ON u.account_id = a.account_id, wk
  WHERE a.current_arr > 0 AND u.week_start IN (wk.cur, wk.prv)
  GROUP BY ALL)
SELECT c.week_start, c.product_name, c.adoption, ROUND((c.adoption - p.adoption) * 100) AS adoption_bps,
       c.gb, p.gb AS prior_gb, ROUND(100 * (c.gb / p.gb - 1), 2) AS gb_wow_pct
FROM m c JOIN m p ON c.product_name = p.product_name AND c.week_start > p.week_start
ORDER BY 2
```

**Q5: Total ARR, NDR and Expansion ARR by tier, with a rollup row.** 4 rows.
```sql
SELECT a.arr_tier,
  COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE a.current_arr > 0) AS accounts,
  SUM(a.current_arr) FILTER (WHERE a.current_arr > 0) AS total_arr,
  ROUND(100.0 * SUM(a.current_arr) FILTER (WHERE a.prior_quarter_arr > 0) / NULLIF(SUM(a.prior_quarter_arr) FILTER (WHERE a.prior_quarter_arr > 0), 0), 2) AS ndr,
  SUM(CASE WHEN a.current_arr > a.prior_quarter_arr THEN a.current_arr - a.prior_quarter_arr ELSE 0 END) FILTER (WHERE a.prior_quarter_arr > 0) AS expansion_arr
FROM accounts a
GROUP BY ROLLUP (a.arr_tier)
ORDER BY a.arr_tier NULLS LAST
```

**Q6: Account-level movement in the two flagged cells, with ARR, renewals and health.** 7 rows.
```sql
WITH cal AS (SELECT MAX(week_start) AS cur, MAX(week_start) + INTERVAL 7 DAY AS as_of FROM weekly_product_usage),
x AS (
  SELECT u.product_name, a.arr_tier, u.account_id,
    SUM(CASE WHEN u.week_start = cal.cur THEN u.ingestion_gb END) AS cur_gb,
    SUM(CASE WHEN u.week_start = cal.cur - INTERVAL 7 DAY THEN u.ingestion_gb END) AS prv_gb
  FROM weekly_product_usage u JOIN accounts a ON u.account_id = a.account_id, cal
  WHERE a.current_arr > 0
    AND ((u.product_name = 'Log Management' AND a.arr_tier = 'Mid-Market') OR (u.product_name = 'Cloud Security' AND a.arr_tier = 'Enterprise'))
  GROUP BY ALL),
c AS (
  SELECT x.*, a.current_arr, a.health_status,
    (a.renewal_date >= cal.as_of AND a.renewal_date < cal.as_of + INTERVAL 90 DAY) AS renew90,
    CASE WHEN cur_gb / prv_gb < 0.10 THEN 'paused (<10% of prior)'
         WHEN cur_gb / prv_gb < 0.85 THEN 'declined >15%'
         WHEN cur_gb / prv_gb > 1.15 THEN 'grew >15%'
         WHEN cur_gb / prv_gb > 1.05 THEN 'grew 5-15%'
         ELSE 'within +/-5-15%' END AS move
  FROM x JOIN accounts a USING (account_id), cal)
SELECT product_name, arr_tier, move, COUNT(*) AS accts,
  ROUND(SUM(prv_gb)) AS prior_gb, ROUND(SUM(cur_gb)) AS cur_gb, ROUND(100 * (SUM(cur_gb) / SUM(prv_gb) - 1), 1) AS gb_pct,
  SUM(current_arr) AS arr, COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE renew90) AS renew90_accts, SUM(current_arr) FILTER (WHERE renew90) AS renew90_arr,
  COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE health_status = 'At Risk') AS at_risk, COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE health_status = 'Critical') AS critical
FROM c GROUP BY ALL ORDER BY 1, 2, 3
```

**Q8: Log Management tickets in the 14-day window, by tier, with overlap against the declining cohort.** 3 rows.
```sql
WITH cal AS (SELECT MAX(week_start) AS cur, MAX(week_start) + INTERVAL 7 DAY AS as_of FROM weekly_product_usage),
x AS (
  SELECT u.account_id,
    SUM(CASE WHEN u.week_start = cal.cur THEN u.ingestion_gb END) / SUM(CASE WHEN u.week_start = cal.cur - INTERVAL 7 DAY THEN u.ingestion_gb END) AS ratio
  FROM weekly_product_usage u JOIN accounts a ON u.account_id = a.account_id, cal
  WHERE a.current_arr > 0 AND u.product_name = 'Log Management' AND a.arr_tier = 'Mid-Market'
  GROUP BY 1),
affected AS (SELECT account_id FROM x WHERE ratio < 0.85),
t AS (
  SELECT t.*, a.arr_tier, (t.account_id IN (SELECT account_id FROM affected)) AS in_cohort
  FROM support_tickets t JOIN accounts a USING (account_id), cal
  WHERE t.product_name = 'Log Management' AND t.created_at >= cal.as_of - INTERVAL 14 DAY AND t.created_at < cal.as_of)
SELECT arr_tier, COUNT(*) AS tickets, COUNT(DISTINCT account_id) AS accts,
  COUNT(DISTINCT account_id) FILTER (WHERE in_cohort) AS cohort_accts_with_ticket,
  (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM affected) AS cohort_size,
  COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE regexp_matches(lower(raw_text), 'overage|log bill')) AS mention_overage,
  COUNT(*) FILTER (WHERE regexp_matches(lower(raw_text), 'renewal|alternatives|leaving')) AS churn_language
FROM t GROUP BY ROLLUP (arr_tier) ORDER BY arr_tier NULLS LAST
```

**Q9: Mid-Market Log Management pain-point buckets.** 5 rows.
```sql
WITH cal AS (SELECT MAX(week_start) + INTERVAL 7 DAY AS as_of FROM weekly_product_usage)
SELECT CASE
    WHEN regexp_matches(lower(raw_text), 'paused|turned off|stopped ingesting|disabled|cut ingestion|cut log volume') THEN '1 cut/paused shipping'
    WHEN regexp_matches(lower(raw_text), 'sampling|throttling|drop rules|pre-ingest') THEN '2 sampling/filtering to stay under quota'
    WHEN regexp_matches(lower(raw_text), 'retention|cold|archive') THEN '3 retention tiering / cheaper storage'
    WHEN regexp_matches(lower(raw_text), 'overage|log bill') THEN '4 overage billing surprise'
    ELSE '5 other' END AS pain,
  COUNT(*) AS n, string_agg(ticket_id, ', ' ORDER BY ticket_id) AS ids,
  arg_min(raw_text, ticket_id) AS example_text
FROM support_tickets t JOIN accounts a USING (account_id), cal
WHERE t.product_name = 'Log Management' AND a.arr_tier = 'Mid-Market'
  AND t.created_at >= cal.as_of - INTERVAL 14 DAY AND t.created_at < cal.as_of
GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 1
```
