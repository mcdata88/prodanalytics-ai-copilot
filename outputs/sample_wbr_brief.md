## Deep Dive: Primary Anomaly & Root Cause

- **Affected Cohort:** Log Management × Mid-Market. **34 of 131** provisioned accounts dropped more than 15% WoW. [Q4]
  - **Paused (11 accounts):** 14,215 GB → 296 GB (−97.9%). Adopted Mid-Market accounts went from 116 to 106 of 131. The paused accounts fell below the adoption threshold, and that drives the −763 bps adoption drop.
  - **Throttled (23 accounts):** 28,476 GB → 14,503 GB (−49.1%).
  - The remaining 97 accounts were flat. Net segment change was −27,694 GB, from 153,855 GB to 126,162 GB.
- **Quantitative Signal:** This is a one-week step change, not a trend. Mid-Market Log Management ingestion held at 152.2K–153.9K GB for the five weeks before 2026-11-02. [Q5]
- **Revenue Exposure:** The 34 accounts carry **$7.57M** current ARR. **19 accounts ($4.81M)** renew within 90 days of 2026-11-09. **26 of 34** are flagged At Risk or Critical by CSMs. [Q7]
- **Qualitative Evidence (Support Tickets):** In the 14 days to the as-of date there were **25 Log Management tickets: 24 Mid-Market and 1 Enterprise**. **22 of the 34 affected accounts filed a ticket** in that window. [Q8] Grouped by pain point:
  1. **Cutting or pausing log shipping to avoid overage (12 tickets).** For example, TKT-1059: *"overage bill this month was nearly 3x our committed spend. We have paused log shipping from staging and non-prod"*. TKT-1067: *"stopped ingesting load balancer and CDN logs until we can renegotiate"*.
  2. **Sampling or throttling to stay under quota (4 tickets).** TKT-1063 and TKT-1086: *"sampling logs at 20% now just to stay within the committed tier"*. Also TKT-1068 and TKT-1069.
  3. **Retention tiering and cheaper cold storage (4 tickets).** TKT-1071 and TKT-1085: *"7 days hot, 90 days cold — at a lower price point"*. Also TKT-1077 and TKT-1088.
  4. **Billing surprise / no budget caps (2 tickets).** TKT-1080 and TKT-1100: *"only found out about the overage when the invoice arrived"*.
  - 6 tickets mention renewal, alternatives, or leaving, for example TKT-1060: *"the log overage charges are the number one objection from our VP Eng"*.
- **Conclusion:** Mid-Market customers are cutting ingestion on purpose in response to Log Management overage pricing. This is not a product defect or a telemetry gap. Enterprise volume is unaffected, which fits Enterprise accounts having larger committed tiers.

## Recommended PM Actions
1. **Immediate — Log Management PM + Mid-Market CS lead:** Run a save play for the **19 accounts renewing within 90 days ($4.81M)**, starting with the 11 that paused shipping. Offer a temporary overage credit or a committed-tier true-up, and give a date for budget caps and pre-ingest drop rules.
2. **Immediate — Pricing / Monetization:** Bring forward scoping of a **hot/cold retention tier** and **ingestion budget alerts**. These map directly to 6 of the 22 overage tickets (4 retention tiering, 2 billing surprise). Another 4 sampling/filtering tickets would be met by pre-ingest drop rules.
3. **Follow-Up Analysis:** (a) Look at the 12 affected accounts with no ticket to see whether they are silent churn risk. (b) Cut Mid-Market accounts by committed-tier headroom to size how many more are near overage. (c) Watch whether SMB Log Management follows next week. It is flat at +0.25% now, but SMB is equally sensitive to price.
4. **Bright-spot follow-up — Cloud Security PM:** Check the +8.45% Enterprise step against the release calendar. If it is launch-driven, it is the expansion story for Q4 QBRs.

All queries were run through `run_readonly_sql` on the `ipa-analytics` MCP server. Governance came from `get_metric_definitions()` (Q0a) and `list_schema()` (Q0b: 3 tables, 500 / 15,432 / 100 rows).

**Q2 — Portfolio scorecard, latest vs prior week** · 4 rows
```sql
WITH wk AS (SELECT MAX(week_start) AS cur, MAX(week_start) - INTERVAL 7 DAY AS prv FROM weekly_product_usage),
m AS (
  SELECT u.product_name, u.week_start,
    ROUND(100.0 * COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN u.is_adopted = TRUE THEN u.account_id END) / NULLIF(COUNT(DISTINCT a.account_id), 0), 2) AS adoption,
    ROUND(SUM(u.ingestion_gb), 2) AS gb
  FROM weekly_product_usage u JOIN accounts a ON u.account_id = a.account_id, wk
  WHERE a.current_arr > 0 AND u.week_start IN (wk.cur, wk.prv)
  GROUP BY ALL)
SELECT c.product_name, c.adoption, ROUND((c.adoption - p.adoption) * 100) AS adoption_bps,
       c.gb, p.gb AS prior_gb, ROUND(100 * (c.gb / p.gb - 1), 2) AS gb_wow_pct
FROM m c JOIN m p ON c.product_name = p.product_name AND c.week_start > p.week_start
ORDER BY 1;
```

**Q4 — Affected Mid-Market Log Management accounts (paused vs throttled)** · 2 rows
```sql
WITH wk AS (SELECT MAX(week_start) AS cur FROM weekly_product_usage),
x AS (
  SELECT u.account_id,
    SUM(CASE WHEN u.week_start = wk.cur THEN u.ingestion_gb END) AS cur_gb,
    SUM(CASE WHEN u.week_start = wk.cur - INTERVAL 7 DAY THEN u.ingestion_gb END) AS prv_gb
  FROM weekly_product_usage u JOIN accounts a ON u.account_id = a.account_id, wk
  WHERE u.product_name = 'Log Management' AND a.arr_tier = 'Mid-Market' AND a.current_arr > 0
  GROUP BY u.account_id),
affected AS (SELECT * FROM x WHERE cur_gb / prv_gb < 0.85)
SELECT CASE WHEN cur_gb / prv_gb < 0.10 THEN 'paused' ELSE 'throttled' END AS grp,
       COUNT(*) AS n, ROUND(SUM(prv_gb)) AS prv, ROUND(SUM(cur_gb)) AS cur,
       ROUND(100 * (SUM(cur_gb) / SUM(prv_gb) - 1), 1) AS pct, SUM(a.current_arr) AS arr
FROM affected JOIN accounts a USING (account_id)
GROUP BY ALL;
```

**Q8 — Log Management tickets, last 14 days, with cohort overlap and pain-point buckets** · 25 / 2 / 5 rows
```sql
WITH wk AS (SELECT MAX(week_start) AS cur FROM weekly_product_usage)
SELECT CASE
    WHEN regexp_matches(lower(raw_text), 'paused|turned off|stopped ingesting|disabled|cut ingestion|cut log volume') THEN 'cut/paused shipping'
    WHEN regexp_matches(lower(raw_text), 'sampling|throttling|drop rules|pre-ingest') THEN 'sampling/filtering to stay under quota'
    WHEN regexp_matches(lower(raw_text), 'retention|cold|archive') THEN 'retention tiering / cheaper storage'
    WHEN regexp_matches(lower(raw_text), 'overage|log bill') THEN 'overage billing surprise'
    ELSE 'other' END AS pain,
  COUNT(*) AS n, string_agg(ticket_id, ', ' ORDER BY ticket_id) AS ids
FROM support_tickets t JOIN accounts a USING (account_id), wk
WHERE t.product_name = 'Log Management' AND a.arr_tier = 'Mid-Market'
  AND t.created_at >= wk.cur + INTERVAL 7 DAY - INTERVAL 14 DAY
GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC;
```
